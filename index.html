How joining works

Every member starts the same way — club leader and admin access is unlocked separately.

Create an account

Sign up with your Coral Shores email. Members can register for tournaments and log catches right away.

Get on the water

Show up to a scheduled tournament, or fish casually and log catches for the season-long species board.

Track the leaderboard

Rankings update the moment a club leader confirms a catch — no waiting for results after the event ends.
